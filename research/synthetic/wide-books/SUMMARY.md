Each paper account: $100 at 10x (capped at the model market's maximum), stops 5% / 10% / 20%. Each scenario is 12 hours (30 minutes of warm-up excluded). CPM = dollars lost per $1M traded (negative = profit). Near BE = projected loss at most 5% of the capital per day. Risk = the second-worst label across scenarios, with the full count.
